# Impeccable: Precision Design Critique and Interface Refinement

Impeccable gives AI agents a rigorous vocabulary and methodology to audit, polish, and harden frontend code.

## Core Commands and Verbs

- `critique`: In-depth analysis of hierarchy, contrast, density, alignment, and emotional resonance.
- `audit`: Checklist validation against WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, responsive breakpoints, and performance budgets.
- `polish`: Elimination of visual friction, aligning pixel edges, tuning border opacities, and smoothing corner radii.
- `clarify`: Rewording dense or confusing UI copy, enhancing microcopy, and eliminating ambiguity in status labels.
- `distill`: Stripping away redundant decorative containers, nested cards, and useless divider lines.
- `typeset`: Calibrating font sizes, line heights, letter spacing, and weights into an intentional scale.
- `quieter`: Reducing visual noise, dimming overly saturated accents, and lowering background contrast.
- `bolder`: Elevating key metrics, establishing undeniable visual anchors, and clarifying primary actions.
- `harden`: Adding robust error boundaries, defensive empty states, loading skeletons, and keyboard trap protection.

## Surface Modes

1. **Persuade & Experience (Landing Pages & Editorial Showcases)**
   - High visual drama, generous vertical rhythm (`py-24` to `py-36`).
   - Engaging interactive storytelling (e.g. 3D Digital Twin, scroll-linked river profiles).
   - High craft in imagery, typographic composition, and subtle lighting.

2. **Operate & Read (Dashboards, Tables, Spatial GIS & Analytics)**
   - High information density, scannable data layouts, strict tabular alignment.
   - Low visual noise: quiet chrome, muted gridlines, crystal-clear labels and units.
   - Immediate feedback on filter updates, sort toggles, and data drill-downs.
